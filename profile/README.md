# Learned Geek

**Learning by building. Solving problems across disciplines.**

Curiosity drives everything. Systems thinking connects it. Continuous learning makes it possible.

### What We Do

We build web applications, APIs, and tools — with a focus on .NET, cloud architecture, and pragmatic engineering. The best solutions come from understanding systems deeply — not just how they work, but why they work.

### Pillars

- **Build to Learn** — Theory informs practice, but practice reveals truth. Real understanding comes from creating, testing, and iterating.
- **Systems Thinking** — Everything connects. The ability to see how parts form wholes is essential to solving complex problems.
- **Continuous Learning** — Expertise isn't a destination. The most valuable skill is the ability to learn new skills.
